> *A hands-on route from amateur to command in computational neuroscience, one build at a time. A route to command, not a degree.*

I'm learning computational neuroscience the way it actually sticks: building the field end to end, one system at a time, with AI as the multiplier that makes real depth possible on my own hours. Not chapters I skim and forget, but working command I can show, and it grows in the open with every build.

## What it covers

Neural models, encoding and decoding on real data, brain-computer-interface decoders, and NeuroAI.

**What it isn't.** Not a degree, and no claim past what each build proves. This is not medical advice: nothing here diagnoses or interprets a clinical finding, human and animal data carry IRB and IACUC duties, and wet-lab and animal work is supervised-only. Know what a dataset actually is before claiming from it.
